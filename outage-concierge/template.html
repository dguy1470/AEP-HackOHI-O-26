How this works, your privacy & responsible use
What’s real
Your area’s past storm damage and the equipment that feeds your power. Not the live grid.
What’s estimated
Risk and repair-time ranges come from past storms. Treat them as rough guides, not promises.
Your part
Follow the checklists and alerts to stay safe and prepared before and during an outage.
🔒 Your privacy
Locations are shown as ~300 m neighborhood areas, not exact homes. IDs are hashed, and we never show names, addresses, or account details — even for life-support locations.
📊 Confidence
Every risk reading shows a confidence level based on how much nearby history supports it.
⚠️ When we’re unsure
If there isn’t enough data for a reliable read, we say so and tell you to check your utility’s official outage map.
